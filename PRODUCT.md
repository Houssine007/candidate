# Product

## Register

product

## Users

HR professionals, recruiters, employees, and candidates of a large competency-driven
organization (framed as IFDC/ONCF in the accompanying memoir). Three authenticated
audiences share one platform:
- **Recruiters / HR / admins** — manage jobs, candidates, the org chart, internal
  mobility, GPEC forecasting, and training. They live in dense dashboards and pipelines.
- **Employees** — view their profile, skill gaps, internal mobility options, and formations.
- **Candidates** — onboard, see matches, and apply.

Users are in a task: sourcing, reviewing pipelines, planning the workforce. The interface
must disappear into the work.

## Product Purpose

RecruitPRO is an all-in-one HR SaaS spanning external recruitment (ATS), internal HR
(employees, org chart, internal mobility, GPEC), and learning (LMS) — three apps unified
by one JWT identity. Success is a recruiter trusting the matching/gap signals at a glance
and an employee seeing a credible path to grow ("savoir-évoluer"). It is the demonstrator
for a strategic competency-management vision.

## Brand Personality

Confident, premium, calm under density. Three words: **trustworthy, intelligent, refined.**
It should feel like an enterprise tool a jury and an HR director both take seriously — not
a flashy startup demo. French-language UI throughout.

## Anti-references

- Generic Bootstrap admin templates (flat gray tables, no hierarchy).
- Trendy AI-generated SaaS slop: gradient-text everywhere, identical icon-card grids,
  hero-metric templates, decorative glassmorphism for its own sake.
- Over-animated, "watch it load" choreography. Users are in flow.

## Design Principles

1. **Preserve the identity.** A committed navy + mint system already exists. Polish
   strengthens it; it does not redesign.
2. **Consistency is the product.** Same affordance, same spacing, same component vocabulary
   screen to screen. Drift is the enemy, not blandness.
3. **Earn trust through clarity.** Hierarchy and legible data over decoration. The signal
   (match score, gap, status) must read instantly.
4. **Accessible by default.** Visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion respected —
   these are correctness, not extras.
5. **Density with breathing room.** Dashboards are dense by necessity; rhythm and spacing
   keep them calm rather than cramped.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1, large/label text ≥3:1, visible `:focus-visible`
indicators with ≥3:1 ring contrast, full keyboard navigation, and a
`prefers-reduced-motion` path for every animation.
